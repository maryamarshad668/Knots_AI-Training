Employees are entitled to 20 paid vacation days per year.
Sick leave requires a doctor's note if absence exceeds 3 consecutive days.
Remote work is allowed up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of purchase.
Employees must complete security awareness training annually.
Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited and will result in termination.
Overtime must be pre-approved by a direct manager before being worked.
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times.
Employees can request parental leave up to 12 weeks, paid at 60 percent salary.
Confidential company data must never be stored on personal devices.
