Our university offers undergraduate and graduate programs in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Information Technology.

The Computer Science undergraduate program has a duration of four years and is divided into eight semesters.

The university library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM from Monday to Friday. On Saturday, it is open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The library remains closed on Sunday.

Students can access academic resources, textbooks, research papers, and digital databases through the university library.

The computer laboratory is available to students from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays.

Students must carry their university identification card when entering the library and computer laboratories.

The university provides student societies for technology, sports, arts, literature, and community service.

Students can join societies by registering through the student affairs office during the society registration period.

The university cafeteria operates from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekdays and provides breakfast, lunch, snacks, and beverages.

The admissions office handles undergraduate and graduate admissions. Students can contact the admissions office for information about eligibility, application deadlines, and required documents.

Students are required to maintain regular attendance in their courses. Attendance requirements may vary depending on the course and university policies.

The university provides academic advising services to help students select courses and plan their academic progress.
